## Justification for V-Model  
The V-Model is suitable for this project due to its structured approach, which aligns with the high regulatory criticality and technical uncertainty. It ensures thorough testing and documentation, critical for compliance with HIPAA and GDPR. Requirements like FR-FIT-004 and NFR-FIT-001 require rigorous security testing and validation, which the V-Model supports.

## Why the Other Top Models Rank Lower  
Waterfall and Spiral models are less suitable due to their linear, rigid approach, which does not accommodate frequent changes or continuous delivery. Agile models, while flexible, lack the formal verification and documentation required for high-risk, regulated systems. The V-Model offers better alignment with the project's complexity and regulatory demands.

## Risks and Mitigation  
- **Regulatory compliance risk**: Ensure adherence to HIPAA and GDPR through formal verification and testing.  
- **Technical uncertainty**: Implement continuous integration and automated testing to manage evolving requirements.
