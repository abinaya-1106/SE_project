# SDLC Analysis

## Characteristics derived from requirements
Here are the ratings and evidence for each item:

1. Requirement stability: Moderate
   Evidence: "The system shall provide a standardized interface for healthcare providers to access patient records." (FR-03) This requirement is not too specific, but also not too vague, indicating a moderate level of stability.

2. Safety or business criticality: High
   Evidence: "The system shall ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data, in accordance with national and international standards." (NFR-01) This requirement is critical to the system's functionality and has significant consequences if not met.

3. Regulatory and documentation burden: High
   Evidence: "The system shall ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data, in accordance with national and international standards." (NFR-01) This requirement explicitly mentions compliance with regulatory requirements, indicating a high level of regulatory burden.

4. System size and complexity: Large
   Evidence: "The system shall support the integration of various healthcare data sources, including laboratory and imaging results." (FR-05) This requirement mentions integration with multiple data sources, indicating a large system size and complexity.

5. Technical uncertainty: Medium
   Evidence: "The system shall be designed to meet the needs of diverse user groups, including government officials, healthcare providers, and patients." (NFR-02) This requirement mentions the need to accommodate diverse user groups, which may introduce technical uncertainty.

6. Need for user feedback during development: Medium
   Evidence: "The system shall provide a user-friendly interface for patients to access their own health records." (FR-08) This requirement explicitly mentions the need for a user-friendly interface, indicating a medium level of need for user feedback during development.

7. Can the system be delivered in parts? No
   Evidence: "The system shall ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data, in accordance with national and international standards." (NFR-01) This requirement implies that the system is a single, integrated entity, rather than a collection of components that can be delivered in parts.

---

Recommended SDLC: Agile

## 1. Justification
The chosen model is Agile due to the volatile nature of the requirements, particularly FR-01, FR-06, and FR-08, which require frequent updates and changes. Additionally, the need for continuous user feedback on the interface and behavior is high, as evident from FR-08. The low regulation and high need for flexibility in the system also support the choice of Agile.

## 2. Why Not the Alternatives
- Waterfall: The requirements are not entirely stable, and the need for frequent updates and changes makes it unsuitable.
- V-Model: Although the requirements are stable, the high criticality and strict regulation of NFR-01 make it a less suitable choice.
- Incremental: The system size and complexity are large, and the need for rapid delivery is not a priority.
- Iterative: The requirements are not entirely known, and the need for rapid delivery is not a priority.
- Spiral: The technical uncertainty is medium, and the system size and complexity are large, making it less suitable.
- Prototyping: The requirements are not entirely unclear, and the need for rapid delivery is not a priority.
- RAD: The system size and complexity are large, and the need for rapid delivery is not a priority.

## 3. Risks and Mitigation
The risks of choosing Agile include:
- Increased complexity and uncertainty
- Higher risk of scope creep
- Potential for delays and cost overruns

To mitigate these risks, the following measures can be taken:
- Regular and frequent team meetings to ensure that all stakeholders are informed and aligned
- Continuous monitoring of the project's progress and adjusting the scope as needed
- Prioritization of the most critical features and delivering them first
- Use of agile tools and methodologies to facilitate collaboration and communication among team members
- Continuous testing and validation to ensure that the system meets the requirements and is free from defects.
