## 1. Functional Requirements

### FR-01
The system shall allow authorized government officials to create, update, and delete patient records.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement is necessary for the government to maintain accurate and up-to-date patient information for administrative and healthcare purposes.

### FR-02
The system shall enable secure and encrypted data transfer between government agencies and healthcare providers.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the confidentiality and integrity of patient data when shared between government agencies and healthcare providers.

### FR-03
The system shall provide a standardized interface for healthcare providers to access patient records.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement facilitates the sharing of patient information between healthcare providers and government agencies, promoting efficient care coordination.

### FR-04
The system shall generate a unique identifier for each patient, linked to their national electronic health record.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement enables the system to track patient information and provide a consistent identifier for healthcare services.

### FR-05
The system shall support the integration of various healthcare data sources, including laboratory and imaging results.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Reasoning:** This requirement allows the system to aggregate and analyze patient data from multiple sources, enhancing the overall understanding of patient health.

### FR-06
The system shall provide a secure and auditable process for tracking patient consent and authorization.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures that patient consent and authorization are properly documented and tracked, protecting patient rights and dignity.

### FR-07
The system shall enable the government to monitor and analyze patient data for public health purposes.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement allows the government to use patient data to inform public health policies and interventions.

### FR-08
The system shall provide a user-friendly interface for patients to access their own health records.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Reasoning:** This requirement enables patients to take control of their own health information, promoting patient engagement and empowerment.

## 2. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01
The system shall ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data, in accordance with national and international standards.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement is essential for maintaining the trust and confidence of patients, as well as complying with regulatory requirements.

### NFR-02
The system shall be designed to meet the needs of diverse user groups, including government officials, healthcare providers, and patients.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures that the system is accessible and usable by a wide range of stakeholders, promoting effective collaboration and care coordination.

### NFR-03
The system shall be able to handle large volumes of patient data, with efficient data processing and retrieval capabilities.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement enables the system to support the growing demands of patient data, ensuring timely and accurate access to information.

### NFR-04
The system shall be designed with scalability and flexibility in mind, to accommodate changing user needs and technological advancements.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures that the system can adapt to evolving user requirements and technological advancements, minimizing the need for costly upgrades or replacements.

## 3. Assumptions and Constraints

This system assumes that the government will provide the necessary infrastructure and resources to support the implementation and maintenance of the national electronic health record system. The system is constrained by the need to comply with national and international standards for data protection and patient confidentiality.

## 4. Overall Reasoning

The requirements for the national electronic health record system were derived from the reference documents by identifying the key functional and non-functional requirements necessary for a secure, efficient, and effective system. The system's design and development will prioritize patient confidentiality, data integrity, and user accessibility, while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and technological advancements.
