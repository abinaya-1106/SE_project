## Justification for Agile-V-Model Hybrid  
Agile-V-Model Hybrid is ranked highest due to the project's high complexity, regulatory criticality, and need for iterative development. This model supports rapid delivery of high-priority requirements like FR-APT-001 (AI validation), FR-APT-002 (human oversight), and NFR-APT-001 (performance scalability). The model's iterative nature aligns with the project's need for continuous delivery and stakeholder involvement, especially for requirements with dependencies (e.g., FR-APT-003 and FR-APT-004). The open issue about data source for AI models (an open risk) is managed through iterative refinement, which Agile-V-Model Hybrid supports. Its flexibility allows for prioritized validation and adaptation, critical for high-consequence requirements like FR-APT-001 and NFR-APT-002.  

## Why the Other Top Models Rank Lower  
V-Model (76%) and Spiral (75%) are less suitable due to their linear, waterfall-like approach, which struggles with the project's high complexity and dependencies. The V-Model requires full documentation and phased delivery, which conflicts with the need for continuous delivery and real-time adjustments (e.g., FR-APT-006). Spiral (75%) is also less flexible, making it harder to address the open issue about data sources for AI models. Agile-DevSecOps Hybrid (68%) and DevSecOps (63%) prioritize security and continuous integration, but they lack the flexibility to handle the project's high regulatory criticality and dynamic requirements. Waterfall (53%) is too rigid, and Agile (41%) lacks the focus on security and compliance needed for this project.
