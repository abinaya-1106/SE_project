## 1. Functional Requirements

### FR-01
The system shall continuously monitor the patient's heartbeat and detect any irregularities or arrhythmias.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement is crucial to ensure the pacemaker's effectiveness and prevent patient harm. It comes from the need for the system to accurately detect changes in the patient's heart rate.

### FR-02
The system shall deliver precise and controlled pulses to the patient's heart, with a maximum deviation of 1% from the prescribed rate.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the pacemaker's reliability and effectiveness in maintaining a stable heart rate. It comes from the need for the system to accurately control the delivery of pulses.

### FR-03
The system shall provide real-time feedback to the patient's medical team, including alerts for any changes in the patient's condition or pacemaker performance.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to communicate critical information to the medical team, enabling timely interventions. It comes from the need for the system to provide situational awareness and support clinical decision-making.

### FR-04
The system shall be able to store and retrieve patient data, including medical history, medication lists, and device settings, for at least 10 years.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to provide a comprehensive view of the patient's medical history and device performance. It comes from the need for the system to support long-term patient care and device tracking.

### FR-05
The system shall be able to update firmware remotely, with a maximum update time of 30 minutes.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to receive timely software updates, ensuring the pacemaker remains secure and effective. It comes from the need for the system to stay current with the latest medical research and device advancements.

### FR-06
The system shall be able to communicate with other medical devices and systems, including ECG machines and hospital information systems.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to integrate with other medical devices and systems, enabling seamless data exchange and coordination. It comes from the need for the system to support interoperability and collaboration.

### FR-07
The system shall be able to detect and respond to device malfunctions or failures, with automatic shutdown and notification to the medical team.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to prioritize patient safety and prevent potential harm. It comes from the need for the system to detect and respond to critical device failures.

### FR-08
The system shall provide a user-friendly interface for medical professionals to configure and monitor the pacemaker, with clear instructions and warnings.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to support user adoption and reduce errors, enabling effective device management. It comes from the need for the system to provide intuitive and user-friendly interfaces.

## 2. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01
The system shall ensure the pacemaker's reliability and effectiveness, with a minimum uptime of 99.9% and a maximum downtime of 1 hour per year.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to maintain a high level of performance and reliability, supporting patient care and safety. It comes from the need for the system to prioritize patient safety and well-being.

### NFR-02

- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to protect sensitive patient information, maintaining confidentiality and integrity. It comes from the need for the system to prioritize patient privacy and data security.

- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to maintain a high level of availability, supporting patient care and safety. It comes from the need for the system to prioritize patient safety and well-being.

- **Reasoning:** This requirement ensures the system's ability to respond quickly and efficiently to patient needs, supporting effective device management. It comes from the need for the system to provide timely and accurate device performance.
