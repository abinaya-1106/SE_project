## 1. Functional Requirements

### FR-01
The system shall allow users to input and manage insulin pump dosages for patients with diabetes.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** Accurate dosing is critical for patient safety and effective diabetes management. This requirement ensures that the system can handle dosage inputs and provide a means for users to manage and track dosages.

### FR-02
The system shall provide real-time alerts and notifications for low blood sugar, high blood sugar, or other critical events related to insulin pump usage.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** Timely alerts and notifications are essential for patient safety and preventing complications. This requirement ensures that the system can provide critical alerts and notifications to users.

### FR-03
The system shall allow users to set and manage insulin pump basal rates, bolus doses, and other settings.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Accurate basal rates and bolus doses are crucial for effective insulin pump therapy. This requirement ensures that the system can handle these settings and provide a means for users to manage them.

### FR-04
The system shall provide a user-friendly interface for patients to view and manage their insulin pump data, including dosing history and trends.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Patients need to be able to access and understand their insulin pump data to make informed decisions about their care. This requirement ensures that the system can provide a user-friendly interface for patients.

### FR-05
The system shall integrate with existing electronic health records (EHRs) systems to ensure seamless data exchange and minimize duplication of effort.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Integration with EHRs systems is essential for ensuring that patient data is accurate, up-to-date, and easily accessible. This requirement ensures that the system can integrate with existing EHRs systems.

### FR-06
The system shall provide a secure and auditable record of all insulin pump dosing and management activities, including user interactions and system events.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** A secure and auditable record of insulin pump dosing and management activities is essential for ensuring patient safety and compliance with regulatory requirements. This requirement ensures that the system can provide a secure and auditable record of all activities.

### FR-07
The system shall support multiple insulin pump devices and protocols, including basal-bolus and pump-free days.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Different insulin pump devices and protocols require different management approaches. This requirement ensures that the system can support multiple devices and protocols.

### FR-08
The system shall provide a means for users to track and manage insulin pump supplies, including cartridge replacements and cleaning schedules.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Accurate tracking and management of insulin pump supplies are essential for ensuring patient safety and minimizing waste. This requirement ensures that the system can provide a means for users to track and manage supplies.

## 2. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01
The system shall ensure that all insulin pump dosing and management activities are performed in accordance with established clinical guidelines and protocols.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** Adherence to clinical guidelines and protocols is essential for ensuring patient safety and effective diabetes management. This requirement ensures that the system can enforce adherence to established guidelines and protocols.

### NFR-02
The system shall provide a secure and auditable record of all user interactions and system events, including login attempts, dosage changes, and alerts.
- **Priority:** High
- **Reasoning:** A secure and auditable record of user interactions and system events is essential for ensuring patient safety and compliance with regulatory requirements. This requirement ensures that the system can provide a secure and auditable record of all interactions and events.

### NFR-03
The system shall ensure that all insulin pump dosing and management activities are performed in a timely and efficient manner, with minimal delays or interruptions.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Timely and efficient dosing and management of insulin pumps are essential for ensuring patient safety and effective diabetes management. This requirement ensures that the system can perform activities in a timely and efficient manner.

### NFR-04
The system shall provide a user-friendly interface that is accessible on a variety of devices, including smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** A user-friendly interface is essential for ensuring that patients and healthcare providers can easily access and manage insulin pump data. This requirement ensures that the system can provide a user-friendly interface that is accessible on a variety of devices.

## 3. Assumptions and Constraints

This software requirements specification assumes that the insulin pump dosing and management system will be used by patients with diabetes and their healthcare providers. The system will be integrated with existing electronic health records (EHRs) systems and will provide a secure and auditable record of all insulin pump dosing and management activities.

## 4. Overall Reasoning

The requirements for the insulin pump dosing and management system were derived from the reference documents by identifying the functional and non-functional requirements that are essential for ensuring patient safety and effective diabetes management. The requirements were prioritized based on their importance and impact on patient care. The system will be designed to meet these requirements, ensuring that it is a safe and effective tool for patients with diabetes and their healthcare providers.
