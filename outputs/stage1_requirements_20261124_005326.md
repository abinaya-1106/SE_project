## 1. Functional Requirements

### FR-USI-001
- **Requirement statement:** The system shall enable AI-based disease detection with clinical validation and integration with healthcare professionals.
- **Category:** Data-management
- **Source stakeholder:** Doctor, Patient, Administrator
- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance criteria:** AI model must be validated by clinical experts and integrated with electronic health records.
- **Applicable regulations:** None identified
- **Risk level:** High
- **Confidence score:** 85
- **Approval status:** Pending human approval
