## 1. Functional Requirements

### FR-FIT-001
- **Requirement statement:** The system shall allow patients to track their fitness and wellness data via a mobile app.
- **Category:** Data-management
- **Source stakeholder:** Patient
- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance criteria:** Patients must be able to view and update their fitness goals and progress.
- **Applicable regulations:** None identified
- **Risk level:** High
- **Confidence score:** 75
- **Approval status:** Pending human approval

### FR-FIT-002
- **Requirement statement:** The system shall provide personalized wellness recommendations based on user preferences and health data.
- **Category:** Wellness
- **Source stakeholder:** Patient
- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance criteria:** The app shall recommend wellness goals based on user input and health data.
- **Applicable regulations:** None identified
