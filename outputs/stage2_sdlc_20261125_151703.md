## Justification for V-Model  
The V-Model is suitable for this project due to its structured approach, alignment with regulatory requirements, and emphasis on formal verification. It supports high regulatory criticality and technical uncertainty, making it ideal for complex systems like medication dose calculation. Requirements like FR-INF-001, FR-INF-002, and NFR-INF-001 require rigorous validation and documentation, which the V-Model supports.

## Why the Other Top Models Rank Lower  
Waterfall and Agile-V-Model Hybrid models are less suitable due to their linear, rigid processes, which struggle with high regulatory criticality and technical uncertainty. Agile models, while flexible, lack the formal verification and documentation needed for high-risk systems, resulting in lower suitability scores.

## Risks and Mitigation  
- **Regulatory compliance risk**: Ensures formal verification and documentation for high-risk requirements.  
- **Technical uncertainty**: Implements continuous delivery and automated testing to mitigate risks.
