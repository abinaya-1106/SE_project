Recommended SDLC: V-Model

## 1. Justification
The V-Model is chosen because the requirements are stable (FR-01, FR-02, FR-03, FR-04, FR-05, FR-06, FR-07, FR-08), and there is a high need for safety and strict regulation (NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-03). The requirement for confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data is critical, and the system must keep working during peak hours with tens of thousands of requests per second (NFR-03). The V-Model is suitable for this project because it ensures that all requirements are traceable to tests and that the system is safe and secure.

## 2. Why Not the Alternatives
The Waterfall model is not suitable because it does not account for the high technical uncertainty and strict regulation in the system (NFR-03). The Incremental model is not suitable because the system is not modular, and the core features are not delivered first. The Iterative model is not suitable because the requirements are mostly known, and the system cannot be delivered in parts. The Spiral model is not suitable because the system is not high-risk, and a risk analysis is not needed in every cycle. The Prototyping model is not suitable because the requirements are not unclear, and there is no strong need for user feedback on the interface or behavior. The RAD model is not suitable because the system is not small or medium, and fast delivery is not the primary goal.

## 3. Risks and Mitigation
The main risk of the V-Model is that it may not account for all the requirements, and the system may not meet the safety and security standards. To mitigate this risk, the project team should:

* Conduct thorough risk analysis and testing to ensure that all requirements are met.
* Use a robust testing framework to validate the system's functionality and security.
* Perform regular reviews and updates to ensure that the system remains compliant with regulatory requirements.
* Use a secure development lifecycle to ensure that the system is secure and protected against unauthorized access.

By following these steps, the project team can minimize the risks associated with the V-Model and deliver a safe and secure system that meets the requirements.
